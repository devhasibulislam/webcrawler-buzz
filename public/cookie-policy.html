This Cookie Policy explains how Domain Crawler uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember information about your visit.

Cookies We Use

Currently, Domain Crawler uses minimal or no cookies. In the future, we may use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function
  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors use our site
  • Advertising cookies — for Google AdSense (future implementation)

Managing Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and set most browsers to prevent them from being placed.
