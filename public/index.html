Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free online website crawler.

Is this web crawler really free?
Yes, WebCrawler.buzz is completely free. There is no signup, no credit card, and no hidden fees. Paste any URL and start crawling instantly.
How many pages can the crawler discover?
There is no hard page limit. The crawler uses a breadth-first approach scoped to the root domain and will continue discovering pages until the entire site is mapped.
Do I need to create an account?
No. You can start a crawl immediately without signing up. You only provide an email if you want to be notified when a large crawl finishes.
How long does a crawl take?
Small sites (under 100 pages) usually finish in under a minute. Larger sites may take several minutes. You get a tracking link so you can close the tab and come back later.
What data does the crawler collect for each page?
For every page discovered, the crawler records the URL, page title, meta description, HTTP status code, response time, content type, word count, and indexability status (noindex, canonical).
Can I export the results?
Yes. After a crawl completes you can export the full report as a CSV spreadsheet. The file is generated and delivered to your email.
Does the crawler respect robots.txt?
Yes. WebCrawler.buzz respects robots.txt directives and will not crawl pages that are disallowed by the site owner.
Can I crawl password-protected or private sites?
No. The crawler only accesses publicly available pages. It cannot log in or bypass authentication.
